Privacy Notice

STI Valencia Sportfest 2026 ID · Supreme Student Council, STI College Valencia

Who is responsible

The Supreme Student Council (SSC) of STI College Valencia processes your data for the STI Valencia Sportfest 2026 event pass, in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), its Implementing Rules, and the school's data privacy policy. The SSC President, Anthony Sedoro, is responsible for handling privacy concerns for this event. You can also reach the SSC at ssc.stival@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Full name, team, role (member or leader), and course & year
  • Your photo, taken or uploaded by you
  • Your access code and ID number (QR)
  • Attendance: date, check-in and check-out times, whether you were on time or late, and which team scanner recorded it
  • Reports you send through "Report a problem", including any contact detail you choose to give

Why we collect it

Only to make your digital STI Valencia Sportfest 2026 event pass, to check attendance during the event, and to handle reports of misused codes. We do not use it for anything else and we do not sell or share it.

How it is handled

  • The face check runs on your own phone. Your photo is not sent to any face-recognition service.
  • Your event pass, a small copy of your photo and your attendance are stored in a Google Drive folder and Google Sheet that belong only to the SSC's official account, ssc.stival@gmail.com. No personal account owns or holds a copy of them. Google may store this data on servers outside the Philippines.
  • At check-in and check-out, your own team's assigned scanners (up to 2 members of your team, set up by the SSC) see your name, team, role and the photo on file, only to confirm it's you. They cannot browse other students.
  • Team leaders receive only the access codes of their own team.
  • The pass you download stays on your phone. Don't share it: your QR code is your entry.
  • We collect only what the pass and attendance need. No phone numbers or addresses are required.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept only until the event is over. STI Valencia Sportfest 2026 runs on November 25, December 2 and December 9. On December 30, 2026, the system deletes your data automatically. No one has to remember to do it.

  • Deleted: your photo, event pass, access code, QR ID, attendance records (check-in/out times and late status), reports you sent, and the scanner accounts.
  • Kept: only the team lists (names, roles, courses and teams), so the SSC knows who joined which team.
  • After the deletion, your old pass and QR code no longer work.
  • The pass you downloaded stays on your own phone. You can delete it yourself any time.
  • Deleted files go to the trash of the SSC's Google Drive (ssc.stival@gmail.com) first and are then emptied for good.

Want your data deleted sooner? Ask the SSC President or use "Report a problem". Your pass will be cancelled and your data deleted.

Your rights

Under RA 10173 you have the right to be informed, to access and correct your data, to object, and to have it deleted or blocked. You may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. To use any of these rights, contact the SSC President, who is responsible for privacy concerns for this event, or use "Report a problem" on this site.

Consent

By ticking the consent box you agree to this processing. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting the SSC; your ID will then be cancelled and your data deleted.

Report a problem

Goes straight to the SSC. Use this if someone used your code, or your details are wrong.
